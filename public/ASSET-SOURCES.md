# Asset provenance

Cost V3: public/flags/us.svg is a local derivative of the retained MIT flag-icons asset. The 50 SVG marker instances were replaced with explicit five-point polygons so stars render consistently in Chromium and Resvg. Stripes/canton colors and flag silhouette remain from flag-icons; original license remains public/flags/LICENSE.txt.

Cost-of-living V2 supersedes the first cost episode's map treatment: original Natural Earth/world-atlas 50m vector paths, local flag-icons SVGs fitted and clipped per land polygon, and matching native 1920×1080 cached holds after the eight-frame reveal fade. No location inset artwork or cost heatmap fills are used in V2.

Cost-of-living episode: original SVG map paths from world-atlas/Natural Earth 1:110m, projected locally; small territories absent from this geometry receive explicit location insets with their licensed flag. The country colors represent the supplied cost estimates. Main typography, eyes and synthesized effects reuse the existing licensed/original project assets.

- Flags: flag-icons 7.5.0, MIT, copyright Panayiotis Lipiridis. License retained at flags/LICENSE.txt.
- Geometry: world-atlas 2.0.2, Natural Earth 1:50m. Country geometry is a cartographic depiction, not a sovereignty claim. Microstates may be absent and require explicit insets in a full episode.
- Background, eyes, HUD: original SVG components in src/WorldMapEpisode.tsx.
- Audio: locally synthesized sine cues using FFmpeg, no reference audio copied. reveal.wav 740 Hz / 120 ms; notice.wav 430 Hz / 220 ms, both faded and played at low volume.

The notice permits personal and commercial use, requires retaining the notice with redistribution, and prohibits selling/modifying the font without permission. Obtained from https://github.com/SyKoHPaTh/python_tutorial_game/tree/dbb93348d3afc3848d34574f0cb2b086a88360f6/assets/Fonts after the user-provided DaFont endpoint returned 403. Font SHA-256: 60ddb78d3a285da2bb7cab2edb27bcf49a5b78180f5e36666fbf7b378dbce29c. Upheaval Pro was not used.

TikTok icon path: Simple Icons, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/tiktok.svg (CC0). Colors styled locally; trademark remains with its owner. Padlock and warning triangle are original SVG drawings.

V2 camera travel uses the original vector map and flags; map PNG tiles are no longer the travel renderer. Cached native-resolution holds remain generated by bake-shots.mjs.

`maps/*.png` and `shots/<episode>/*.png` are generated locally from the existing world-atlas 50m geometry and flag-icons SVG assets by scripts/bake-map.mjs and scripts/bake-shots.mjs. No borders or flags are generated by an image model. Rasterizer: @resvg/resvg-js (MPL-2.0); see its package license. Derivatives retain the source-map and flag attributions above/below. These are cached image layers, not independently sourced maps or evidence of application access.
